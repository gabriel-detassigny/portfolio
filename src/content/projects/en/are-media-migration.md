## The situation

Are Media, an Australian publisher and an XWP client, wanted 12 of its sites, with millions of users between them, moved onto a single WordPress multisite.

I was the product owner, working with a team of five developers, a project manager, a QA engineer and a designer.

## The approach

- **A year-long launch calendar:** I sequenced the launches by each site's features, the client's priorities and the team's availability, often with several sites in progress at once.
- **Smooth switch-overs:** each site was switched over to the multisite without disrupting its readers.
- **Quality at every launch:** accessibility, and Core Web Vitals kept in the green.
- **Stakeholder feedback:** I prioritized feedback from client stakeholders into the backlog and managed expectations about what would ship and when.

## Result

All 12 sites moved to the multisite.
